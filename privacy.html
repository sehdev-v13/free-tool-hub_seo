Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how FreeToolHub handles information when you use this website.

Browser-based tools

Many FreeToolHub tools perform calculations directly in your browser. For example, image compression is designed to process the selected image in the browser rather than requiring an upload to a FreeToolHub server.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled, third-party advertising providers such as Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements according to their policies and your settings. The site may be updated with the applicable advertising disclosures after an advertising account is approved.

Contact

If you have a privacy question, use the contact page.
