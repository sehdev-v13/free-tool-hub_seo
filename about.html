About FreeToolHub

FreeToolHub is a collection of simple online tools for everyday calculations and small business tasks. The tools are designed to be quick, mobile-friendly and easy to use.

Our tools

The website includes calculators and utilities for GST, EMI, percentages, invoices, UPI QR codes, image compression, age calculations and unit conversion.

Accuracy and verification

These tools are intended as convenient calculation aids. For important financial, tax or payment decisions, verify results against official documents or professional advice.
